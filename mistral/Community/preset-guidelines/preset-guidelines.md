# Purpose

This document defines how reusable instruction presets should be designed, evaluated, maintained, and continuously improved for AI-assisted engineering, product development, technical writing, business strategy, documentation, reviews, and operational workflows.

A preset is not a prompt.

A preset is a standardized operating guideline that establishes consistent objectives, decision-making principles, quality expectations, constraints, and execution behavior across multiple tasks.

The objective is to create reusable instruction systems that produce predictable, reliable, maintainable, and high-quality outcomes regardless of task complexity.

Good presets improve consistency.

Not creativity at the expense of quality.

# Definition of Done

Preset review is complete when

- The purpose, scope, objectives, constraints, and intended audience are clearly defined.
- Decision-making rules consistently guide execution while allowing appropriate professional judgment.
- Quality standards, workflows, governance, and documentation provide predictable and repeatable outcomes.
- The preset can be reused across multiple projects, teams, and future revisions without significant modification.
- Versioning, ownership, review processes, and continuous improvement ensure long-term maintainability.
- The preset reduces ambiguity, improves collaboration, and strengthens consistency across engineering, product, business, and operational activities.
- Contributors can understand, apply, and extend the preset without requiring additional explanation.
- Validation confirms that the preset consistently produces reliable, high-quality outcomes across representative scenarios.
- Organizational standards, business objectives, and engineering best practices remain aligned throughout the preset lifecycle.
- The preset functions as a reusable operational standard that continuously improves execution quality, organizational consistency, and long-term knowledge sharing.

Exceptional presets become organizational standards.

They reduce uncertainty, improve decision quality, accelerate execution, preserve institutional knowledge, and enable teams to consistently deliver high-quality work regardless of project complexity or contributor experience.
