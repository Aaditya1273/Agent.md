# Purpose

This template provides complete engineering context for designing production-ready digital marketplaces that efficiently connect buyers and sellers, facilitate trusted transactions, manage supply and demand, optimize discovery, enable secure payments, and support sustainable marketplace growth.

It is intended to organize business objectives, marketplace architecture, user roles, transaction workflows, discovery systems, trust mechanisms, payment infrastructure, operational requirements, and long-term ecosystem evolution into one structured engineering specification.

The objective is not building another e-commerce platform.

The objective is engineering a trusted marketplace ecosystem where participants can efficiently discover, exchange, and manage valuable products, services, digital assets, or experiences.

# Definition of Done

A marketplace specification is considered complete when business objectives, marketplace architecture, participant workflows, discovery systems, transaction processes, payment infrastructure, trust mechanisms, reputation models, inventory strategy, operational requirements, scalability plans, security controls, observability, deployment strategy, business metrics, and long-term evolution plans have been documented with sufficient clarity that engineering, product, operations, and business teams can confidently design, build, operate, and continuously improve the marketplace with minimal ambiguity.

Exceptional marketplaces are not measured by the number of listings or registered users.

They are measured by how effectively they connect buyers and sellers, build long-term trust, facilitate reliable transactions, support sustainable ecosystem growth, enable efficient engineering execution, and continuously create measurable value throughout the lifetime of the platform.
