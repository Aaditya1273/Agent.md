# Purpose

This document defines how Gemini should design, evaluate, optimize, and continuously improve sustainable product growth strategies for SaaS platforms, AI applications, developer tools, APIs, startups, marketplaces, enterprise software, and digital businesses.

Growth is not acquiring more users.

Growth is the systematic process of increasing customer value, adoption, retention, expansion, referrals, revenue, and long-term business sustainability.

The objective is to build repeatable, measurable, scalable growth systems that continuously compound business value while improving customer success.

Growth is a system.

Not a campaign.

# Definition of Done

Growth review is complete when

- Business objectives, customer outcomes, and sustainable growth goals are clearly defined.
- Customer acquisition, activation, retention, expansion, and referral systems operate as measurable, repeatable processes.
- Product experience minimizes friction and enables users to reach meaningful value as quickly as possible.
- Growth initiatives improve customer success while strengthening long-term revenue, profitability, and retention.
- Product-led growth, growth loops, referrals, and expansion opportunities continuously compound business growth.
- Analytics provide complete visibility into acquisition efficiency, activation, engagement, retention, expansion, and customer lifetime value.
- Experiments continuously validate assumptions and optimize every stage of the customer lifecycle.
- Documentation captures strategies, experiments, metrics, processes, and organizational learning.
- Operational systems scale alongside customer growth without reducing product quality or customer experience.
- Growth becomes an integrated business capability that continuously creates value for customers while increasing revenue, market position, and long-term competitive advantage.

Exceptional growth is invisible.

Customers naturally discover the product, experience value quickly, remain successful, expand their usage over time, recommend it to others, and create a self-sustaining system where customer success and business growth reinforce each other indefinitely.
