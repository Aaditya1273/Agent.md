# Purpose

This document defines how Kimi should design, evaluate, optimize, and continuously improve monetization strategies for SaaS platforms, AI products, developer tools, APIs, marketplaces, mobile applications, enterprise software, open-source projects, and digital businesses.

Monetization is not charging customers.

Monetization is the systematic process of capturing fair economic value from products while maximizing customer success, sustainable profitability, long-term retention, and business growth.

The objective is to create monetization systems that align business incentives with customer outcomes, ensuring that revenue grows as customers become more successful.

Customers should feel that paying creates more value than it costs.

# Definition of Done

Monetization review is complete when

- Customer value, business impact, and willingness to pay are clearly understood.
- Revenue models align naturally with how customers receive value from the product.
- Pricing, packaging, and feature allocation create clear upgrade paths without artificial frustration.
- Free offerings build trust, accelerate adoption, and encourage sustainable conversion to paid plans.
- Enterprise monetization addresses the operational, security, compliance, and scalability requirements of larger organizations.
- Billing, payments, renewals, refunds, and subscriptions provide a transparent and frictionless customer experience.
- Revenue analytics, unit economics, retention, expansion, and profitability are continuously measured and optimized.
- Customer success, product quality, and long-term relationships remain the primary drivers of revenue growth.
- Documentation clearly defines monetization principles, pricing logic, billing policies, financial metrics, and operational processes.
- The monetization system enables sustainable business growth by ensuring that customers perceive increasing value as their investment grows.

Exceptional monetization never feels like extracting money.

It feels like a fair exchange where customers willingly invest more because the product continuously delivers greater value, stronger outcomes, and measurable business success.
