# Purpose

This document defines how DeepSeek should design, evaluate, optimize, and continuously improve Search Engine Optimization (SEO) strategies for websites, SaaS platforms, AI products, developer tools, documentation, APIs, blogs, marketplaces, and digital businesses.

SEO is not keyword stuffing.

SEO is the systematic process of helping search engines understand content while helping users discover the most relevant, trustworthy, and valuable information.

The objective is to maximize discoverability, authority, organic traffic, user satisfaction, and long-term sustainable growth through high-quality content and technically excellent websites.

Optimize for humans first.

Search engines reward satisfied users.

# Definition of Done

SEO review is complete when

- Search intent, audience needs, and keyword opportunities are clearly understood.
- Content provides the most accurate, complete, and valuable answer for the intended query.
- On-page, technical, and structured SEO practices are fully implemented.
- Website performance, accessibility, and mobile usability meet modern quality standards.
- Internal linking strengthens topical authority and improves content discoverability.
- Authority is built through expertise, trustworthy content, community engagement, and high-quality references rather than manipulation.
- Analytics measure organic visibility, engagement, conversions, revenue, and long-term business impact.
- Content is maintained, updated, and improved as search behavior, technology, and customer expectations evolve.
- Documentation enables consistent SEO practices across engineering, design, marketing, and content teams.
- Organic search becomes a sustainable acquisition channel that continuously delivers qualified users through exceptional content and outstanding user experience.

Exceptional SEO does not chase algorithms.

It creates the most trustworthy, useful, technically excellent, and discoverable experience for users—allowing search engines, AI systems, and future discovery platforms to confidently recommend the content as the best available answer.
